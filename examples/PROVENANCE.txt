Source: user-supplied Persistent Cross Entropy manuscript, Figures 1 and 2,
Appendix B.1-B.2 and Tables 3-4 (PDF supplied on 2026-10-13).

Figure 1 geometry was extracted from the final original two-loop notebook.
Final generator parameters are recorded explicitly in figure1_two_loops.py.
Figure 2 simulation, embedding and alpha-complex conventions were extracted
from the original alpha-complex spring-mass notebook. No notebook is executed
at runtime. Experimental source files and historical results remain unchanged.

All paper PCE metrics use tpce.entropy with g(l)=l/(1+l), Gaussian similarity,
fixed figure-specific bandwidths and a single globally calibrated response
scale. Probabilities retain the unexplained event and are not normalized over
reference bars. These paper experiments do not use adaptive pair bandwidths.

The exact 2-Wasserstein baseline uses SciPy's linear sum assignment instead
of GUDHI/POT: same order 2, Linfinity birth-death ground metric, and diagonal
matching. Uniform silhouette weights use the arithmetic mean of tents.
At alpha=0, beta=.4, all six tested symmetric baselines matched saved original
results to at most 1.4e-15 (both coordinate directions).

Generated PNG previews come from the supplied runnable scripts. The original
manuscript PDF, datasets, manuscript sources, PRL snapshots and research output
folders are not part of this repository.
